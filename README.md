# End-to-End Demand Forecasting & Business Decision Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end business analytics and forecasting platform that transforms historical demand data into forecasts, anomaly alerts, what-if scenarios, and actionable business decisions.

##  Business Problem

Businesses need to answer:

- How much demand should we expect?
- Which periods require additional inventory?
- Are there unusual demand spikes or drops?
- What happens if demand increases by 10%, 15%, or 20%?
- How can forecasting support inventory and operational decisions?

This project addresses these questions through an end-to-end analytical pipeline.
